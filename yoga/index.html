THE PHILOSOPHY

A union of mind, body & spirit

Patanjali's Yoga Sutras describe eight interwoven limbs, known together as Ashtanga, that guide a practitioner from outer conduct toward inner absorption. Together they form a single path, not eight separate steps.

01

Yama

Ethical restraints, how we relate to the world.

02

Niyama

Personal observances, how we relate to ourselves.

03

Asana

Steady, comfortable posture, the body as vessel.

04

Pranayama

Breath control, the bridge between body and mind.

05

Pratyahara

Withdrawal of the senses, turning attention inward.

06

Dharana

Concentration, resting the mind on a single point.

07

Dhyana

Meditation, an unbroken flow of attention.

08

Samadhi

Absorption, union with the object of meditation.

THE ASANA DIRECTORY

Five poses to begin with

Every posture is an inquiry into balance, breath, and where the body still holds tension.

Beginner

Tree Pose

Vrikshasana

Roots the standing leg while the raised foot presses into the thigh, cultivating balance, focus, and quiet steadiness.

Beginner

Downward-Facing Dog

Adho Mukha Svanasana

An inverted V that lengthens the spine and hamstrings while energizing the whole body, often used as a resting pose between flows.

Intermediate

Triangle Pose

Trikonasana

A standing stretch that opens the hips and chest while strengthening the legs and core through a long lateral line.

Beginner

Cobra Pose

Bhujangasana

A gentle backbend that opens the chest and shoulders while building strength along the spine.

Advanced

Dancer's Pose

Natarajasana

A deep balancing backbend requiring flexibility through the shoulders and spine, and unwavering focus underfoot.

Find your pace

Every body arrives at a posture differently. Progress is not the goal. Presence is.
